# 🌾 Agri-Medic: Smart Assistance for Sustainable Farming

Agri-Medic is a web-based smart farming assistance platform designed to help farmers identify and resolve crop-related issues by connecting them with agricultural experts and nearby pesticide shop owners. The system promotes sustainable farming by enabling timely expert guidance and access to the right agricultural products.

---

## 📌 Purpose of the Project

In many rural areas, farmers face challenges such as:
- Crop diseases and pest attacks  
- Lack of timely expert consultation  
- Difficulty in finding suitable pesticides and nearby shops  

**Agri-Medic** bridges this gap by providing a digital platform where farmers, experts, and shop owners collaborate to solve farming issues efficiently.

---

## 💡 Project Overview

Agri-Medic connects **Farmers**, **Agricultural Experts**, and **Shop Owners** on a single platform:

- 📷 **Farmers** upload crop images and describe problems  
- 🧑‍🌾 **Experts** analyze issues and recommend appropriate pesticides or sprays  
- 🏪 **Shop Owners** provide the recommended products and usage instructions  
- 📚 Farmers can also read expert blogs and farming tips shared on the platform  

---

## ⚙️ System Modules

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Module
- Register / Login  
- Upload crop images and issue descriptions  
- View expert recommendations  
- View suggested pesticide shops  

### 🧑‍🔬 Expert Module
- Analyze crop-related issues  
- Recommend pesticides or sprays  
- Publish farming blogs and tips  

### 🏪 Shop Owner Module
- View expert recommendations  
- Provide required products  
- Share usage and safety guidelines  

### 🛡️ Admin Module
- Manage Farmers, Experts, and Shop Owners  
- Monitor platform activities  
- Generate reports
